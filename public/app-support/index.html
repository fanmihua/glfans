资料如何更新？

App 会通过 HTTPS 自动检查公开资料更新，也可以手动刷新。新的内容经校验后整组更新本地缓存；网络或校验失败时保留已有资料。若更新未完成，请确认网络可用后再刷新。

已缓存在设备上的内容可以离线阅读；尚未缓存的图片及外部链接需要网络。资料更新与 App 版本更新是两件事：功能改进仍可能需要通过 App Store 更新应用。

追剧选择会同步到别处吗？

语言和追剧列表保存在当前设备，不会上传给 glfans，也没有跨设备账号同步。换设备或卸载后可能需要重新选择。播出安排可能调整；如发现信息有误，请附上剧名、日期和可核对的公开来源。

表情包无法保存怎么办？

保存到系统照片需要“仅添加照片”权限。若曾拒绝授权，请在系统设置中找到 glfans 并检查照片权限，再返回 App 重试。App 不需要读取你的照片图库；不授权也可继续浏览。

What is available?

The App has four sections: the drama archive, CP profiles, REPO articles and memes. The broadcast calendar is available from the archive and related works. No account registration is required. The App currently has no literature section, comments, submissions, likes or radio.

How does content update?

The App automatically checks for public content updates over HTTPS and supports manual refresh. A validated content set replaces the local cache as a group. If the network or validation fails, the existing content remains available. Check your connection and try refreshing again if an update does not finish.

Previously cached content can be read offline. Images that have not been cached and external links require a network connection. Content updates are separate from App updates; new features may still require an update through the App Store.

Are followed shows synchronized?

Your language choice and followed shows stay on the current device and are not uploaded to glfans. There is no account-based cross-device sync. You may need to choose them again after changing devices or uninstalling the App. Broadcast schedules can change. To report an error, include the show, date and a verifiable public source.

Why can’t I save a meme?

Saving an image to Photos requires add-only photo permission. If you previously declined it, find glfans in system settings, check its photo permission and try again. The App does not need to read your photo library. You can continue browsing without granting permission.

You can also use GitHub issues for general reports. Issues may be public; use email for requests involving personal information.
