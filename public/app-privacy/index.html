隐私政策

这里说明 glfans App 使用哪些数据、为什么使用，以及你可以如何管理这些数据。

生效及更新日期：2026 年 9 月 17 日

1. 适用范围

本政策适用于 glfans App 及为其提供公开内容更新的 glfans.com 服务。App 提供考古档案、播出日历、百家饭、REPO 和表情包；当前没有账号注册、评论、投稿、点赞、音频播放或广告追踪功能。

2. 保存在设备上的数据

语言选择、追剧列表及相关浏览偏好保存在当前设备。App 不会把这些选择上传给 glfans，也不提供账号或跨设备同步。公开内容及图片可缓存在设备上，以便再次阅读。

你可以在 App 中更改语言和追剧选择；卸载 App 可移除其本地数据。你已保存到系统照片中的图片需在照片 App 中自行管理。

3. 内容更新与服务日志

App 通过 HTTPS 自动检查公开内容更新，也允许手动刷新。更新下载的是网站公开资料，校验后整组更新本地内容缓存；更新失败时保留可用的旧资料。此过程不会上传语言选择或追剧列表。

设备访问 glfans.com 时，服务器会产生访问日志；出现服务异常时可能产生错误日志。日志可能包含 IP 地址、请求路径、请求时间、响应状态及用于排查问题的技术信息，用于服务运行、故障排查和安全维护。我们不使用这些日志进行广告追踪，也不出售这些数据。

服务日志仅在提供服务、排查问题和维护安全所需的期限内保留；法律要求保留的情形按相应要求处理。本政策不承诺固定的日志保存天数。

4. 照片权限

当你选择把表情包保存到系统照片时，App 会请求“仅添加照片”权限，用于保存你选择的图片。App 不读取你的照片图库，也不需要相机或麦克风权限。你可以拒绝此权限，其他阅读功能仍可使用；也可以随时在系统设置中管理授权。

5. 外部链接与联系信息

作品来源、官方账号、视频等链接可能打开第三方网站。这些网站按各自的隐私政策处理数据；点击前请留意目标地址。

如果你通过邮件联系维护者，我们会使用你提供的邮箱及消息内容回复请求。若使用 GitHub 提交公开问题，相关内容可能公开可见；请勿填写密码、身份证件或其他不必要的个人信息。

6. 你的请求与政策更新

如需询问数据处理方式、更正联系信息，或提出与个人信息有关的请求，请联系 fan422601@gmail.com。我们会根据请求内容及适用要求进行处理。App 功能或数据处理方式改变时，会同步更新本页面及上方日期。

Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: September 17, 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to the glfans App and the glfans.com service that supplies its public content updates. The App includes the drama archive, broadcast calendar, CP profiles, REPO articles and memes. It currently has no account registration, comments, submissions, likes, audio playback or advertising tracking.

2. Data stored on your device

Your language choice, followed shows and related browsing preferences stay on the current device. The App does not upload these choices to glfans or offer account-based or cross-device synchronization. Public content and images may be cached on the device for later reading.

You can change your language and followed shows in the App. Uninstalling the App removes its local data. Images you have saved to the system photo library must be managed separately in Photos.

3. Content updates and service logs

The App automatically checks for public content updates over HTTPS and also supports manual refresh. It downloads the website’s public information and replaces the local content set after validation. If an update fails, the previously available content remains usable. This process does not upload your language choice or followed shows.

Requests to glfans.com generate server access logs, and service problems may generate error logs. These may contain IP addresses, request paths, request times, response status and technical information used to diagnose problems. They support service operation, troubleshooting and security. We do not use them for advertising tracking or sell this data.

Service logs are retained only for the period needed to operate the service, investigate problems and maintain security, or when retention is required by law. This policy does not promise a fixed retention period.

4. Photo permission

When you choose to save a meme to Photos, the App requests add-only photo permission to save the selected image. It does not read your photo library or require camera or microphone permission. You may decline permission and continue using the reading features, or manage the permission in system settings.

5. External links and support messages

Source references, official accounts and video links may open third-party websites. Those websites handle data under their own privacy policies. Please check the destination before opening a link.

If you email the maintainer, we use the email address and message you provide to respond. GitHub issues may be publicly visible. Do not include passwords, identity documents or unnecessary personal information.

6. Requests and policy changes

For questions about data handling, corrections to contact information or personal-data requests, email fan422601@gmail.com. Requests will be handled according to their circumstances and applicable requirements. When App features or data practices change, this page and its update date will be revised.

Third-party materials remain the property of their respective rights holders. Rights-related concerns may be sent through the GitHub rights feedback form or the email above.
